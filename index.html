WITHHOLDING TRACKER

What being a non-filer costs you.

On a bank cash withdrawal over Rs 50,000 a day, a filer pays 0% and a non-filer pays 0.6% (sample rates from the FBR rate card — verify current rates on fbr.gov.pk). Ahmed Raza made 4 withdrawals of Rs 100,000 in November: as a non-filer he paid Rs 2,400; a filer would have paid Rs 0. His year-to-date sample loss is Rs 18,600.

AGENT SPOTTER

Spot the agent traps.

Four things an agent does that should stop you paying him — and one safe habit. An agent charging Rs 5,000–15,000 to file a return you can file free on IRIS in about 30 minutes; a "guaranteed ATL in 24 hours" promise when ATL updates weekly; asking for your IRIS password or OTP; and an SMS saying "FBR: your ATL is cancelled, pay here" with a link. Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.

LATE-FILING EXPLAINER

The deadline just passed. Here is what it means.

The Tax Year 2026 return was due 30 Sep 2026, extended twice to 31 Oct 2026 (Circulars 3 & 4 of 2026-27, Section 214A). There was no third extension. FBR received 7.3 million returns by 29 Oct 2026. Returns filed from 1 Nov 2026 are late — and rejoining the Active Taxpayers List costs an individual Rs 25,000 (Section 182A).

What to do now

File the late return on IRIS, pay the Rs 25,000 ATL surcharge, and check the Active Taxpayers List a week later. Then put the next deadline in your calendar: the Tax Year 2027 return is due 30 Sep 2027.
